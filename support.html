Help & getting started

Keep your game moving.

Setup tips and answers for Karete Punch.

Start a camera round

  1. Place your iPhone upright, about 1–2 metres away, with room to move.
  2. Choose Flow, Rush, or Zen. Tap “Let’s move” and allow camera access.
  3. Raise a hand in good light. Gameplay starts immediately when it is detected.
  4. Make a fist and punch, or sweep an open hand across a nearby target. A colored ring shows when a target is in reach.
My punches are not registering

Keep your wrist and knuckles visible, use even lighting, and avoid covering one hand with the other. Strike targets when their reach ring appears. Adjust gesture sensitivity in Settings. Touch practice can help you learn the timing.

The camera preview disappeared

The small preview hides after five seconds. Tap the camera button at the bottom right to show it again. Hand tracking continues while it is hidden.

What happens when my hands leave the view?

The round continues: the timer, targets, and misses keep advancing. Your virtual hands reappear when the camera detects them. Use Pause when you want a break.

Camera access is denied

Open your iPhone’s Settings, find Karete Punch under Apps, and enable Camera. You can also play touch practice without camera access.

How do I finish Zen mode?

Tap Pause, then “Finish this round.” You can resume a paused round even when your hands are not visible.

Contact support

Use the support form to report a problem or ask a question. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and the steps that caused the issue.

Open a support request

The support form uses GitHub and requires a GitHub account. Requests are public. Please do not include passwords, personal contact details, or camera images.
