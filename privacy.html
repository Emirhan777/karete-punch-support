Your privacy.

This policy explains how Karete Punch for iPhone, provided by Emirhan Şimşek, handles information.

Camera and hand tracking

Camera access is used to detect hand positions for gameplay. Camera frames and hand landmarks are processed on your device. The app does not record, save, or upload camera images or hand-tracking data. Microphone access is not used. You can disable camera access in iOS Settings and use touch practice instead.

Data stored on your device

The app stores your personal best score and preferences, such as sound, haptics, and gesture sensitivity, using local iOS app storage. The app does not transmit this information to the developer. Device backups and restores are controlled by your Apple settings.

No in-app tracking or account

Karete Punch does not require an account. It has no advertising, third-party analytics SDK, tracking identifier collection, or developer-operated server. The app does not sell personal information.

Support requests

If you choose to contact support through GitHub, the information you submit is used to respond to your request. GitHub issues are public, so avoid including personal information. You control the content you submit through your GitHub account.

This website

This support website is hosted by GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics scripts or advertising cookies. GitHub may process technical information about visits, including IP addresses and request information, under its privacy statement.

Your choices

You can revoke camera permission in iOS Settings. Deleting the app removes its local app data from the device; backups are managed separately through Apple. For questions about information you have shared in a support request, contact us through the support form.

Changes and contact

Updates to this policy will appear on this page with a revised effective date. For privacy questions, contact support on GitHub.
