A movement game for iPhone

Punch. Chop.
Find your rhythm.

Your hands enter a world of smooth shapes. Punch forward, sweep across, and build your streak.

How to play

Flow

A relaxed 60-second round to find your rhythm.

Rush

Faster targets and 90 seconds to keep the streak alive.

Zen

No time limit. Play at your pace and finish from the pause menu.

Raise a hand. Start playing.

Stand your iPhone upright, allow front-camera access, and show your hands. The round starts as soon as a hand is detected. Prefer touch? Tap to punch and swipe to chop in touch practice.

Your camera stays yours.

Hand tracking runs on your iPhone. The app does not record or upload camera frames, and it has no account, ads, or analytics SDK.

Read the privacy policy

For iPhone running iOS 17 or later. Camera play needs a front camera and good lighting.
